# News Source and Framing Playbook

Reviewed on 3 August 2026 from the recent visible posts on:

- https://x.com/thetatvaindia
- https://x.com/indiain24hr

These profiles are discovery references, not standalone verification. Every selected story still requires corroboration from an official or reputable independent source.

## Observed patterns

### The Tatva

- Leads with a short alert marker such as `SHOCKING` or `WATCH`.
- States the core event in one compact, fast-scanning sentence.
- Mixes event video with designed image cards depending on the story.
- Favors high-clarity civic, unusual, achievement, business, and breaking-event stories.
- Uses strong verbs and puts the most visual fact first.

### India in Last 24hr

- Uses a category-led format such as `VANDALISM`, `HEROIC RESCUE`, or `SHOCKING`.
- Usually follows with city and state, then the event, immediate consequence, and authority or attribution.
- Writes more context than The Tatva and often includes impact or police response.
- Favors highly visual breaking incidents, rescues, public disorder, accidents, and viral events.

## TezScroll selection model

Use the strongest parts of both approaches:

1. Category or alert label.
2. Exact location when verified.
3. Subject plus central action.
4. Immediate consequence or public relevance.
5. Attribution and corroboration.

Prefer a story when it is current, credible, understandable immediately, visually usable, relevant to a general Indian audience, and suitable for the existing TezScroll layout.

Avoid copying sensational wording when facts are uncertain. Never infer motive, identity, blame, casualty numbers, or legal status beyond verified reporting.

## Mandatory sensitivity gate

Before downloading or editing any video, mark whether the story contains any of the following:

- A minor is involved or identifiable.
- Blood, a graphic wound, a body, or severe visible injury appears.
- Sexual content, sexual violence, abuse, exploitation, or intimate imagery is involved.
- Self-injury, attempted suicide, suicide, or a closely related act is involved.

If any item is true:

- Do not download, edit, repost, or publish the incident video.
- Generate a respectful, non-graphic contextual image.
- Avoid showing the harmful act, injury, weapon in use, sexualized detail, identifiable minor, or method of self-harm.
- Add `Illustrative image` to the post or caption.
- Retain normal source attribution for the underlying news facts.
- Never describe the generated image as real footage or an on-scene photograph.

If none is true, video is only permitted after source, date, context, rights, quality, and visual relevance checks pass.

## Headline structure

- Exactly three visible lines.
- Five to six simple words per line when the layout permits.
- Line 1: subject and central action.
- Line 2: location or immediate context.
- Line 3: consequence or public relevance.
- Direct and attention-grabbing, but never more dramatic than the verified facts.

## Canva layer structure

- The new video or image must be the bottom media layer, matching the approved visual reference.
- Breaking label, three-line headline, red highlight shape, TezScroll logo, date, and all other design elements must sit above it.
- Delete every previous foreground and background media element before inserting the replacement.
- Never crop the source image or video.
- Lock the aspect ratio and scale the complete media proportionally to the 1080 x 1350 page.
- For a 4:5 asset, use Width 1080 px and Height 1350 px.
- Align Left and Top; verify X 0 px, Y 0 px and rotation 0 degrees.
- Fill the frame without borders or distortion while retaining the complete source.
- Open Position -> Layers and verify the order before export.

## Caption structure

1. What happened, who was involved, and where.
2. Impact, response, investigation, safety issue, or wider relevance.
3. Original source and visual source.
4. Fair-use and public-content disclaimers.
5. Rights, credit, and removal contact note.

For a generated visual, explicitly add: `Visual: Illustrative image generated for contextual representation; not authentic incident footage.`
