## Source caption

The United States has issued sweeping security alerts across the Middle East, asking American citizens in several Gulf and regional countries to consider leaving or remain prepared to depart at short notice amid fears of another sudden escalation in the Iran war.

US embassies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Iraq, Israel, Jordan and Lebanon released similar advisories on Saturday. The alerts described the security situation as “fluid” and warned that Iran’s actions remained unpredictable, with attacks increasingly reaching locations that had previously avoided direct strikes.

“Americans in the region should consider departing, or be prepared to depart should there be escalation,” the embassies stated. Those outside the Middle East were also advised to seriously reconsider travelling to or transiting through the region.

The warnings followed US President Donald Trump’s threat that Washington would continue striking Iran “very hard” until Tehran could no longer sustain the conflict. American citizens were asked to maintain heightened vigilance, check flight operations and prepare for cancellations, temporary airspace closures and other major travel disruptions.

The advisory also warned that US diplomatic facilities, companies and institutions could become targets for Iran or Tehran-aligned armed groups. Americans in Israel were advised to locate nearby bomb shelters, while those in Jordan were told to avoid US military bases following recent Iranian missile attacks.

However, the situation changed hours later when Trump announced that he would hold off on fresh attacks after Iran and unnamed Middle Eastern countries reportedly requested additional time to negotiate an agreement. Trump said the proposed deal would require the complete reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and an end to Iran’s nuclear threat.

Tensions nevertheless remain extremely high. Kuwait recently intercepted Iranian drones targeting vital facilities, while separate maritime incidents were reported near Oman. Tehran has warned that countries assisting any new American or Israeli attack could face retaliatory strikes.
